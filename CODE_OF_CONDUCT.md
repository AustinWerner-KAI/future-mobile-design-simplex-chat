# Community conduct

Critique the design and its effects on people. Make room for different skills, languages and access needs. Explain disagreement with concrete examples. Credit others' work.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, publication of private information and sustained personal attacks are unacceptable. Moderators may remove material, close a thread or restrict participation, with a short explanation where appropriate.

Initial maintainer: [AustinWerner-KAI](https://github.com/AustinWerner-KAI). A dedicated private project reporting contact has not yet been published. For abuse on GitHub, use GitHub’s report-abuse route; avoid posting sensitive personal details in public issues. Routine project moderation requests can identify the affected thread without exposing private information.
