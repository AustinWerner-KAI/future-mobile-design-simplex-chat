# Five starting challenges

Each challenge should become a separate issue after launch. Sketches and small experiments are welcome.

## A home that feels continuous

**Problem:** the prototype home is still a stack of rows.
**Deliverable:** two alternatives with one opening/closing motion study.
**Check:** find Maya, an unread conversation and a shared plan; explain a path to 100+ conversations. Names remain stable and readable. Include reduced-motion behaviour.

## Tools at the point of action

**Problem:** tools should unfold from their real origin, within reach.
**Deliverable:** Write → Photo → preview → back, preserving the draft.
**Check:** one-handed reach, keyboard visibility, cancellation, 200% text size and labelled tap alternatives.

## A photograph you can discuss

**Problem:** anchored replies need a reliable coordinate and accessible alternative.
**Deliverable:** image reply placement, editing and a text-based list of annotations.
**Check:** aspect ratios, image zoom, screen-reader navigation and actual image bounds instead of letterboxed margins.

## A boundary people understand

**Problem:** people may confuse invitation, membership and agreement.
**Deliverable:** sender and recipient journeys with a comprehension test.
**Check:** before send, the person identifies content and audience; private history stays outside the new space; joining does not imply date confirmation.

## One relationship, distinct channels

**Problem:** chat and email can appear together while obscuring delivery and privacy differences.
**Deliverable:** one person's mixed conversation with an explicit reply-channel choice.
**Check:** sender address, subject, threading, forwarding, attachment scope, duplicate identities and failed delivery. Do not invent an email security guarantee.
