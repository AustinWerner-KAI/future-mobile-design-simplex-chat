# Design memory: a different evolutionary path

Project: **From anatomy to interface**. Maintainer direction recorded 4 October 2026.

> “NEVER forget the Octopus theory and its different evolution tree — Simplex.chat is that for networks and chat protocols.”

This is the central premise of the project. The octopus followed a different evolutionary path from humans. The design analogy is that SimpleX's network and chat protocols provide different starting conditions from the assumptions behind mainstream messaging products. We are exploring what an interface might become when it grows from those conditions.

This is Austin’s design thesis, not an official SimpleX statement or roadmap. Evolution is not a ladder of progress. Do not claim that the octopus is the most evolved animal, the most distant animal from humans, universally communal, or literally has nine independent brains. Keep the useful analogy without turning metaphor into biological evidence.

## What must survive every iteration

1. **Different foundations must change behaviour.** No required global user identifier, contextual identity, deliberate connections and understandable membership should affect how people enter and move through the experience. Consult the [SimpleX research](SIMPLEX_RESEARCH.md); do not invent protocol capabilities.
2. **The relationship coordinates.** Keep the source conversation and intended audience understandable as tools unfold.
3. **Action is local.** Put image discussion, plans and replies beside the content they concern. An extra destination must earn the navigation cost.
4. **Visibility is deliberate.** People choose when to reveal previews, identity and shared content. Camouflage inspires disclosure control, not deceptive or automatically disappearing controls.
5. **Form adapts to purpose.** Space follows attention. Quiet conversations stay compact; the active task can unfold. A radial eight-arm menu is not a requirement.
6. **Design has no ego.** Novelty, motion and organic shapes must serve clarity, not decorate the theory. Preserve legibility, touch targets, keyboard access and reduced motion.
7. **Explore 2028.** Current features are foundations, not the entire brief. Shared objects, anchored media and email are hypotheses with explicit uncertainties, not claims of implemented SimpleX functionality.
8. **Invite others into the thinking.** Preserve earlier iterations, questions, critiques and failed directions. Give contributors a specific assumption to challenge.

## The design test

For every new concept, answer: what comes from SimpleX’s foundations; what comes from the octopus analogy; what changes for the person; what evidence could make us abandon it?

Reject a concept that relies only on “fluid”, “futuristic”, blue gradients or rounded cards to explain its value. Equally, do not reject an effective list just because it is familiar. The evolutionary difference must be expressed in the experience, not forced into every silhouette.

The [complete experience](../experience.html) explores nine connected contexts. The compact menu and ink/porcelain/cyan hierarchy are the current visual direction, not immutable rules. ## Research anchor and spatial consequence

The [expanded project map](SIMPLEX_PROJECT_MAP.md) grounds the different-evolution analogy in resource-based network addressing and endpoint application logic. The [surface audit](MOBILE_SURFACE_AUDIT.md) applies it to space: ordinary conversation remains the coordinating context; the active tool earns its surface; explanations unfold on request; simulation controls stay outside the proposed app.
