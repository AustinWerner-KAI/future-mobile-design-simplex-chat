# The Future of Mobile Design for SimpleX Chat — build with us

We are exploring what private messaging could feel like in 2028. Our starting idea comes from the octopus: local action, flexible form and coordinated movement.

A conversation opens into a workspace. Tools appear where they are needed. Images become places to discuss details. Sharing boundaries remain visible. Returning should feel calm and preserve your place.

We have a working study and several unresolved problems. The home still resembles a list. Scale, accessibility, real protocol permissions and mixed chat/email behaviour need careful work.

You can help with a sketch, an observation, a motion study, a usability session or a small prototype. Start with one of the five challenges. Show what changes for the person using it and how we can assess the difference.

SimpleX is our first design case. This is an independent community exploration, not an official redesign. Contributions should improve the experience and provide useful evidence that maintainers could consider.
