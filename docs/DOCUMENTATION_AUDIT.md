## Findings corrected

- Current inline and research copy still described warm paper, sage or blanket blue rejection. Updated the current direction to white, subtle blue surfaces, neutral words, oat email and clay actions.
- Six-theme/Plum and earlier palette judgments could read as current guidance. Labelled the superseded steps and retained their sequence.
- Broad design principles could be mistaken for Jony Ive’s documented theory. Attributed them to this project and linked Apple only for historical context; no Ive approval or direct quote is asserted.
- Upstream `stable` citations could drift. Pinned code/privacy references to the reviewed full commit IDs; mutable website sources retain an access date and limitation.
- Earlier browser pass counts lacked a shared evidence map. Linked retained scripts/data and identified temporary checks/logs that were not preserved. This audit does not recertify historical runs.
- Visual attribution needed complete coverage. Added a file/hash inventory for every asset under `visuals`, while preserving missing prompt/capture records and rights uncertainty.
- Earlier web wireframes blurred CSS target sizes with native targets. Clarified the units and linked WCAG/source guidance.

Its article content was independently retrieved and read through the browsing tool; the exception is recorded in [source-link-check.json](source-link-check.json). Additional primary context sources added during the review were opened through the browsing tool. Reachability is not a claim-verification method by itself.

The asset register covers **76 files** under `visuals`. Hashes verify identity, not copyright. Missing exact prompts for seven original boards and incomplete early capture/test logs remain explicit. No complete public originating transcript or independent rights verification is asserted.

After edits, 377 local documentation targets resolved and all 76 registered asset hashes matched. No factual accuracy, accessibility, privacy or security guarantee follows from this review. Design value, native feasibility and participant outcomes remain open. Future factual changes need a new source check; proposals need new evidence, not just another citation.
