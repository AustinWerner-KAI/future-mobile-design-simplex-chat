## Design principles this version holds to

These are the rules the [Kings of Mobile Design framework](MOBILE_AUDIT_LOG.md) applies and that the checks enforce. Each one is stated, then where it is tested.

- **Human relationships organise the interface; the octopus theory is the flow.** Every new surface was checked against coordinate, act locally, adapt space, retain context, cross deliberately and return coherently. The octopus checks are written in [T08](T08_IDENTITY.md#octopus-flow-check), [T19](T19_CHANNEL_DISCOVERY.md) and [T11](T11_EMAIL_HANDOFF.md).
- **Identity is named only at the point of action (E1).** Not in the channel header, not on the home row, not in the profile mark's visible text. It appears at the comment crossing, the invitation, the acceptance line and the composer. Tested in `check-identity.cjs` and `check-channel-discovery.cjs`.
- **One defining moment.** Visual expression stays on the deliberate crossing: the 3 px clay rule. Everything else is quiet. No decorative motion was added.
- **Colour is never the only cue.** Entity type is carried by shape and badge; selected states by a check mark and weight; the self mark by fill and a spoken label. Measured contrast: mark 11.7:1, muted text 6.7:1, badge 12.5:1, primary button 6.9:1. No blue words.
- **Targets 44 px or more, text 12 px or more.** Every scanned state at 320 and 390 wide. Physical size on real phones is an open item (E6).
- **Words stay whole at large text.** `overflow-wrap: break-word` on bodies, flex rows with `white-space: nowrap` for tabs and chips, round controls clamped 44–56 px, avatars capped at 56 px. Tested at 32 px root text on 320 wide in the runtime, both studies and the profile sheet.
- **One shape scale.** Circle, pill, 12, 16, 6, 5. Every radius in the runtime and the studies is asserted against the scale; a new radius fails the build.
- **Anchored tiles.** A tile in a multi-column grid shows its edge or centres its content. Asserted on every grid in the runtime.
- **State is RAM only.** Nothing persists across reload. Per-profile state is swapped wholesale on a switch so that nothing crosses without an audit of every reference.
- **Measure first, fix the shared stylesheet, add an assertion that fails on the pre-fix build.** Every fix on 5 October 2026 followed this order; a fault the owner sees more than once becomes a framework rule (words whole, anchored tiles).

## What this version does not establish

- Human comprehension. Nothing here has been used by a participant. [T05](T05_PROTOCOL.md) is ready and decides: whether people find mid-screen tools, whether a monogram identifies a profile, whether the audience is read correctly, whether corner targets are reachable.
- Native behaviour: keyboards, Dynamic Type and font scale, VoiceOver and TalkBack, safe areas on hardware.
- Upstream facts still open: [five maintainer questions](MAINTAINER_QUESTIONS.md) on link prefetch, link contents, read-marking, cross-profile forwarding and the directory data source.
- Open design items: IA1–IA4 and IA6 naming and URL questions; D3 and D6 in the discovery study; E6 corner target size; the profile edit screen; T06, T07, T09 and T10.

## Editing and rebuilding

Edit `prototypes/src/v21/app.js`, `style.css` and `index.html`, then run `node scripts/build-demo.mjs`. Never edit `v21.html` alone. `v2121.html` and `v21-options.html` are directly authored galleries and keep the earlier radii until re-captured. Add a check for every behaviour you change; make it fail on the pre-fix build before you trust it.
