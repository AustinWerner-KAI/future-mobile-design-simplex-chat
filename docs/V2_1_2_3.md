## The idea

The octopus followed a different evolutionary journey from us. simplex.chat has no user IDs and keeps contacts and groups on the device, so this project asks whether its interface could grow from its own foundations instead of copying WhatsApp and Telegram.

**Human relationships define what the interface contains. The octopus theory informs how the experience flows.** The six flow rules are in [EVOLUTION_FROM_ANATOMY](EVOLUTION_FROM_ANATOMY.md): coordinate, act locally, adapt the working space, retain useful state, cross deliberately, return coherently. It is a design hypothesis to test, not a description of SimpleX's protocol or a biological simulation.

## Design principles this version holds to

- **Human relationships organise the interface; the octopus theory is the flow.** Every new surface was checked against the six flow rules.
- **Identity is named only at the point of action (E1).**
- **Tools sit where you act.** The camera beside the reply, the pin on the photo, the forward inside the email. Nothing takes over the screen without a way back.
- **Channels stay distinct inside one relationship.** Chat and email never blur; anything that leaves SimpleX says so before it goes.
- **Honest states.** Queued, accepted by a server and delivered are different words. A failure says what did not happen and offers a next step.
- **One defining moment, one motion.** Visual expression stays on the deliberate crossing. The only motion is the 200 ms slide between home and a conversation, transform and opacity only, and none at all with reduced motion.
- **Colour is never the only cue. No blue words.**
- **Targets 44 px or more, text 12 px or more, words whole at large text.**
- **One shape scale:** circle, pill, 12, 16, 6, 5.
- **State is RAM only.** Nothing persists across a reload.
- **Measure first, fix the shared source, add a check that fails on the pre-fix build.**

## What this version does not establish

- Human comprehension. Nothing here has been used by a participant. [T05](T05_PROTOCOL.md) is ready and needs six to nine people on their own phones.
- Native behaviour: keyboards, Dynamic Type and font scale, VoiceOver and TalkBack, safe areas, real animation timing.
- Upstream facts: [five maintainer questions](MAINTAINER_QUESTIONS.md) remain unanswered.
- Open design items: IA1–IA4 and IA6; D3 and D6; E6 corner target size; the profile edit screen; T06 links between entities, T07 group lifecycle, T10 recovery after reload; tablet layout; pin clustering; real address books.

## Editing and rebuilding

Edit `prototypes/src/v21/app.js`, `style.css` and `index.html`, then run `node scripts/build-demo.mjs`. Never edit `v21.html` alone. `showcase.html` is authored directly. Add a check for every behaviour you change and make it fail on the pre-fix build before you trust it. Put audit detail in the [running audit log](MOBILE_AUDIT_LOG.md).
