# S1: community survey draft (not yet posted)

> Independent design study, not affiliated with SimpleX. Draft, 7 October 2026. Kai decides whether, where and when to post it.

**Purpose.** Size the collection lab's assumptions before T05: how many chats people use, whether they already organise them, how they find someone, what text size they use, and whether they use a screen reader. Read with [the collection lab](collection-lab.html) and `docs/RESEARCH_NOTES/README.md`.

**Where.** A public SimpleX community group or the SimpleX subreddit, as a single post. Replies in the thread, or a privacy-respecting form with no sign-in and no tracking. Aim for 30 or more replies; fewer is still a signal, not a measure.

**Privacy.** No names, contact links, screenshots of chats or device identifiers are asked for. Every question is optional. Answers are reported only as counts.

---

## The post

> **Five quick questions about finding people in SimpleX (2 minutes, all optional)**
>
> I'm running an independent design study on how SimpleX could show the people and chats you use most. It is not affiliated with the SimpleX team. Please don't share names or anything that identifies you or your contacts. Reply with the question numbers and your answers.
>
> 1. **About how many chats do you open in a normal week?** 1 to 5 · 6 to 15 · 16 to 40 · more than 40
> 2. **Do you use chat lists or favourites?** No · Favourites only · My own lists (how many?) · Both
> 3. **How do you usually find someone you want to message?** Scroll the chat list · Search · Favourites or a list · Something else (what?)
> 4. **What text size is your phone set to?** Default · Larger · Largest · Not sure
> 5. **Do you use VoiceOver, TalkBack or another screen reader with SimpleX?** No · Sometimes · Always
>
> Optional: if you'd try a 30-minute test of a prototype on your own phone, with fictional people only, say so and I'll get in touch here. [Kai: add how people reach you]

---

## How the answers change the lab

| Answer | What it would change |
|---|---|
| Most open 1 to 5 chats a week | A small grid is enough; the All tile is rare. Test the grid against the plain chat list in T05 |
| Many open more than 40 | Lists and search matter more than any home strip; raise the priority of list performance (CHAT_SCROLLING.md) |
| Few use favourites or lists | "Keep close" may not be found; T05 must test whether people set it up at all |
| Many use larger text | Make the large-text suggestion more prominent, or start in the list for them |
| Any screen reader users | Invite them to T05 first; H0 becomes the lead result in the pitch |

## Bias and limits

- People who answer community posts are more engaged than most users.
- Self-reported counts are rough.
- The question order goes from neutral to specific so earlier answers don't steer later ones.
