Channels arrive through people.

How someone goes from the home screen to a channel page: finds, previews, joins and reads a SimpleX channel without losing where they started. Fictional channels and people. Static mockup; nothing is sent, joined or paid for.

Journey: from the home screen to the channel page

Other doors to the same preview

1From a person

A link shared in a chat or group. Most aligned with "who you engage with".

2By name

"Follow #coastjournal". Typed into home search.

3From a forwarded post

Forwards hide the source, so the sharer has to choose "Share channel".

4By topic

An explicit step into the curated directory.

5From the web

A link opens the same preview in the app.

Hypothesis for T19, ranked by fit with the relationship-first thesis, not by measured behaviour. Channels are SimpleX v6.5 beta. Community Credits and public names are announced, not live, and are not simulated here. Subscriber counts, people and channel names are fictional. Test with participants in T05 before building further.
