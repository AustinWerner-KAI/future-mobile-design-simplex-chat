One human model.
Three ways to find your world.

Each direction starts with people and entities. The octopus architecture governs what follows: local tools, retained context, deliberate sharing and a coherent return.

Four recognisable identities

Individuals use one circular identity. Groups use a cluster of people. Publications use a page-shaped mark. Providers use a storefront. Text labels reinforce the differences; audience and transport remain explicit inside each workspace.

Candidate options, not validated outcomes. Shared fictional fixtures and local simulations. No real QR data in identity marks. Reload clears state. V2 remains the current project reference.
