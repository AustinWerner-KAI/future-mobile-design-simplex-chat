The idea that holds the project together

Different foundations. A different evolution.

The octopus is our design analogy, not a shape to copy. Local action, adaptable form and selective visibility help us question inherited messaging conventions. SimpleX’s network and chat protocols are the starting conditions; these designs explore what might evolve from them.

Local action → tools beside contentCoordination → relationships stay in viewSelective visibility → deliberate disclosureAdaptability → space follows attention

An independent design thesis, not SimpleX’s official roadmap or a claim that one animal is “more evolved.”
